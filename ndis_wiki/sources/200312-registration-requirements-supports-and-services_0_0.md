Registration Requirements by Supports and Services

The supports and services that NDIS providers are registered to deliver or are seeking to
deliver determines the NDIS Practice Standards that an NDIS provider must undertake an
assessment against.

The NDIS Practice Standards consist of a core module and several supplementary modules
that apply according to the types of supports and services NDIS providers deliver.

Supports and services

The National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA) works with NDIS participants to develop
individual plans to access supports and services that are considered ‘reasonable and
necessary’ to help achieve the person’s goals. Providers must be registered to deliver
supports and services to NDIS participants who have their plan managed by the NDIA. The
support items available to be provided by providers under the NDIS is determined by the
NDIA with each support item and service grouped under ‘Registration class/groups’. The
NDIS Commission is responsible for assessing the quality and competency of a provider
through it’s registration function.
