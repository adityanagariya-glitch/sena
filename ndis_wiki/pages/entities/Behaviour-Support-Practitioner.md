# Behaviour Support Practitioner

A **Behaviour Support Practitioner** is a specialist who works with [[Participant]]s to develop strategies that improve their quality of life and reduce the need for [[Restrictive-Practices]].

## Roles and Responsibilities
- Conducting functional behaviour assessments.
- Writing and reviewing [[Behaviour-Support-Plan]]s.
- Providing advice and training to [[Provider]]s and [[Worker]]s.

## Suitability
- Practitioners must be considered suitable by the [[NDIS-Commission]].
- They are assessed against the **Positive Behaviour Support Capability Framework**.
- As of June 2021, there were 6,686 practitioners in Australia.
