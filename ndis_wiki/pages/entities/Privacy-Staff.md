# Privacy Champion & Officers

These are key internal roles within the [[NDIS-Commission]] responsible for data protection and [[Privacy]] compliance.

## Privacy Champion
- Usually the Director of the Privacy team.
- Promotes a culture of privacy and provides strategic leadership.
- Reports to the executive on privacy issues.

## Privacy Officers
- Assistant Directors and Senior Review Officers.
- Responsible for the day-to-day handling of privacy enquiries and complaints.
- Conduct **Privacy Impact Assessments (PIA)** for new projects.
