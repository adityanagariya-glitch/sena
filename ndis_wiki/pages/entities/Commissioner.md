# Commissioner

The **Commissioner** is the accountable authority and head of the [[NDIS-Commission]].

## Role
- Appointed under the NDIS Act.
- Responsible for the core functions of the Commission (Registration, Complaints, Behaviour Support).
- Inaugural Commissioner: **Graeme Head AO**.

## Powers
The Commissioner has a range of statutory powers including issuing [[Compliance-Notice]]s, [[Banning-Order]]s, and initiating investigations into serious breaches of the [[Code-of-Conduct]].
