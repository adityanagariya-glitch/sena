# NDIS Commission Staff

**NDIS Commission Staff** are the employees of the [[NDIS-Commission]], regulated under the Australian Public Service (APS) framework.

## Growth & Culture
- The agency grew significantly between 2019 and 2020 (from 181 to 291 staff surveyed).
- Staff are surveyed annually via the [[APS-Census-2019]] and [[APS-Census-2020]].
- Wellbeing and workload capacity were major challenges identified during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## Responsibilities
Staff are responsible for the regulation of [[Provider]]s, management of [[Complaints-Management]], and oversight of [[Reportable-Incidents]].
