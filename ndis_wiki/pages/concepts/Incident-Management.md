# Incident Management

**Incident Management** is the system used by [[Provider]]s to identify, respond to, and report incidents that occur during the delivery of NDIS supports.

## Obligations
- Registered providers must have a formal incident management system.
- Certain incidents (e.g., death, serious injury, [[VANE]], [[URP]]) are **Reportable Incidents** and must be notified to the [[NDIS-Commission]].
- The system must focus on the safety and wellbeing of [[Participant]]s and prevent recurrence.
