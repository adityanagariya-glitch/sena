# Behaviour Support Plan

A **Behaviour Support Plan** is a formal document designed to respond to the needs of a [[Participant]] and reduce the causes of [[Behaviours-of-Concern]].

## Purpose
- To provide a roadmap for reducing or eliminating the use of [[Restrictive-Practices]].
- To improve the quality of life for the participant.
- Must be written by a qualified **Behaviour Support Practitioner**.
