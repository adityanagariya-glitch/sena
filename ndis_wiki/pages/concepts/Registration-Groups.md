# Registration Groups

**Registration Groups** (or classes of support) are the specific categories of services that a [[Provider]] is authorized to deliver.

## Significance
- A provider's registration groups determine which [[Practice-Standards]] modules they must meet.
- They dictate the type of [[Audit]] (Verification or Certification) required.
- Certain groups (e.g., [[Behaviour-Support]], Specialist Housing) have high-intensity requirements.

## Examples
Common groups include Therapeutic Supports, Household Tasks, and Early Intervention.
