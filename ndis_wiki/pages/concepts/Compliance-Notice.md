# Compliance Notice

A **Compliance Notice** is a formal regulatory tool used by the [[NDIS-Commission]] to compel a [[Provider]] to fix issues of non-compliance.

## Features
- It is **non-punitive** (it doesn't fine the provider immediately).
- It specifies the exact steps a provider must take to become compliant.
- Failure to comply with the notice is a serious breach and can lead to court action or revoking registration.

## Relation to Other Tools
It is often used as a "middle-tier" tool in the [[Compliance-Enforcement-Policy]] pyramid, between education and heavy enforcement like [[Banning-Order]]s.
