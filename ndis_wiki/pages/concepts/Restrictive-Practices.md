# Restrictive Practices

**Restrictive Practices** are interventions that have the effect of restricting the rights or freedom of movement of a [[Participant]].

## Regulated Restrictive Practices
The NDIS regulates five types of restrictive practices:
1. **Seclusion:** Solitary confinement.
2. **Chemical Restraint:** Medication for behavior control.
3. **Mechanical Restraint:** Devices to restrict movement.
4. **Physical Restraint:** Use of force.
5. **Environmental Restraint:** Restricting access to the environment.

## Compliance
- Must be used only as a last resort.
- Must be part of a [[Behaviour-Support-Plan]].
- Must be authorized by the relevant state/territory body.
- Unauthorized use is a [[URP]] and must be reported.
