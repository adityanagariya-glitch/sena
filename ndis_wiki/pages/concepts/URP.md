# Unauthorised Restrictive Practices (URP)

An **Unauthorised Restrictive Practice (URP)** occurs when a [[Restrictive-Practices]] is used without the required state/territory authorization or without being part of an active [[Behaviour-Support-Plan]].

## Reporting Obligations
- [[Provider]]s must report **every single instance** of a URP to the [[NDIS-Commission]] as a reportable incident.
- This results in very high notification numbers (over 1 million in FY20-21).
- Reporting must continue until the practice is authorized and a plan is lodged.

## Common Types
- **Chemical Restraint:** Use of medication to influence behaviour.
- **Environmental Restraint:** Restricting access to the environment.
- **Mechanical Restraint:** Use of devices to restrict movement.
- **Physical Restraint:** Use of physical force.
- **Seclusion:** Confining a person alone in a room.
