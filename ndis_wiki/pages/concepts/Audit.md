# Audit

An **Audit** is a formal check of an NDIS [[Provider]]'s performance and records against the [[Practice-Standards]].

## Types of Audits
- **Verification Audit:** A lighter-touch audit for lower-risk/complexity services.
- **Certification Audit:** A comprehensive audit for higher-risk or complex services.

## Scope
Audits check how well a provider works, their records, and the quality of experience for [[Participant]]s.
