# NDIS Quality and Safeguarding Framework

The **NDIS Quality and Safeguarding Framework** is the national policy document that underpins the operations of the [[NDIS-Commission]].

## Core Components
- **Developmental:** Strengthening the capability of [[Participant]]s, [[Worker]]s, and [[Provider]]s.
- **Preventative:** Using tools like [[Practice-Standards]], the [[Code-of-Conduct]], and [[Worker-Screening]] to prevent harm.
- **Corrective:** Managing [[Complaints-Management]], [[Reportable-Incidents]], and enforcement actions.

## Governance
Developed by the Council of Australian Governments (COAG) Disability Reform Council to ensure national consistency in disability support safety.
