# Regulatory Priorities

**Regulatory Priorities** are the annual focus areas published by the [[NDIS-Commission]] to signal where it will direct its monitoring and investigation resources.

## Purpose
- To inform [[Provider]]s and [[Worker]]s of emerging risks.
- To prioritize the safety of [[Participant]]s in high-risk settings (e.g., home care, restrictive practices).
- To respond to external crises (e.g., [[COVID-19-Notification-Notice]]).

## Historical Context
The priorities for 2021-22 shifted focus heavily towards **URPs** and **Mealtime Safety**.
