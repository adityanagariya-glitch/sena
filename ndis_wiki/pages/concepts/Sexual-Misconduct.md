# Sexual Misconduct

**Sexual Misconduct** is a serious breach of the [[Code-of-Conduct]] and is strictly prohibited in the NDIS.

## Definition
Includes any sexual act, contact, or behavior that is non-consensual, illegal, or occurs within a professional relationship where there is an imbalance of power.

## Obligations
- [[Provider]]s and [[Worker]]s must take all reasonable steps to prevent and respond to sexual misconduct.
- Any allegation or suspicion of sexual misconduct is a **Reportable Incident** and must be notified to the [[NDIS-Commission]] within 24 hours.
