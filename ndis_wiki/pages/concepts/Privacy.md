# Privacy

**Privacy** is a human right and a core element of the [[Code-of-Conduct]].

## Obligations
- [[Provider]]s and [[Worker]]s must comply with Commonwealth and State/Territory privacy laws.
- Personal information (name, address, health details) must be protected.
- Services must be delivered in a dignified way that maintains personal privacy (e.g., during showering).

## Disclosure
Information can be disclosed without consent in specific cases, such as mandatory reporting of [[VANE]] or [[Sexual-Misconduct]].
