# Prohibited Restrictive Practices

**Prohibited Restrictive Practices** are specific interventions that are considered human rights infringements and are banned across NDIS services.

## Categories
1. **High-Risk Physical Restraint:** Includes prone and supine restraints, which can be fatal.
2. **Punitive Measures:** Any practice intended to punish rather than support (e.g., aversive practices, denial of needs).
3. **Degradation:** Practices that are demeaning or vilifying.

## Regulation
These practices are distinct from **Regulated Restrictive Practices**, which may be used if authorized and part of a [[Behaviour-Support-Plan]]. Prohibited practices must **never** be used.
