# Behaviour Support

**Behaviour Support** focuses on improving the lives of people with disability and reducing [[Behaviours-of-Concern]].

## Key Elements
- **Behaviour Support Practitioner:** A specialist who writes [[Behaviour-Support-Plan]]s.
- **[[Behaviour-Support-Plan]]:** A document with steps to improve behaviour and reduce the use of [[Restrictive-Practices]].

## Regulation
Providers delivering specialist behaviour support must meet specific supplementary modules of the [[Practice-Standards]].
