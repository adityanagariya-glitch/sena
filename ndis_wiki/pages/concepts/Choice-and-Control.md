# Choice and Control

**Choice and Control** is a core principle of the NDIS, rooted in the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities.

## Definition
The right of [[Participant]]s to make their own decisions about who supports them and how their supports and services are delivered.

## Practical Application
- [[Worker]]s should assume participants have the capacity to make choices.
- Providers must support "Supported Decision Making" for those who need it.
- Communication must be in a form the participant understands.
