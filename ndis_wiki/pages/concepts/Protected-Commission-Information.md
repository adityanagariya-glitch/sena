# Protected Commission Information

**Protected Commission Information** is a legal term defined under the NDIS Act for information held by the [[NDIS-Commission]] that is subject to strict secrecy provisions.

## Scope
Includes personal information about [[Participant]]s, [[Worker]]s, and [[Provider]] records, excluding what is already public on the NDIS Provider Register.

## Legal Protection
- Unauthorized use or disclosure of this information is a **criminal offence**.
- It requires a higher level of protection than standard personal information under the Privacy Act.
