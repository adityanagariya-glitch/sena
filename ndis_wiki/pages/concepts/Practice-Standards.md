# NDIS Practice Standards

The **NDIS Practice Standards** specify the quality and safety requirements that registered [[Provider]]s must meet.

## Structure
- **Core Module:** Mandatory for most providers, covering rights, governance, and the support environment.
- **Supplementary Modules:** Required for high-risk or complex services (e.g., [[Behaviour-Support]], Specialist Housing).

## Assessment
Providers are assessed against these standards via an [[Audit]] process (either Verification or Certification).
