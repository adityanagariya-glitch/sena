# Violence, Abuse, Neglect, and Exploitation (VANE)

People with disability are at a greater risk of experiencing **VANE**. The [[Code-of-Conduct]] requires proactive prevention and response.

## Definitions
- **Violence:** Physical or sexual violence.
- **Abuse:** Psychological, emotional, or financial harm.
- **Neglect:** Passive or willful deprivation of care (e.g., heat stress).
- **Exploitation:** Unfairly taking advantage of a [[Participant]].

## Reporting
All incidents must be reported to the [[NDIS-Commission]] via the [[Incident-Management]] system.
