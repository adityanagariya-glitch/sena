# Summary: Compliance Notices Policy (January 2022)

## Overview
Provides guidance on the use of **[[Compliance-Notice]]s** as a non-punitive tool to address identified or suspected non-compliance with the NDIS Act.

## Key Takeaways
- **Definition:** A written direction requiring a [[Provider]] to take (or refrain from taking) specific actions within a timeframe.
- **Legislative Basis:** Section 73ZM of the NDIS Act.
- **Purpose:** To encourage quick changes in conduct without immediate legal proceedings.
- **Failure to Comply:** Can lead to [[Infringement-Notice]]s, civil penalties, or suspension/revocation of registration.

## Appropriateness
Likely used when there is no history of non-compliance, but a significant risk to the health and safety of [[Participant]]s exists.
