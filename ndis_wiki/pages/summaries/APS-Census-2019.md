# Summary: APS Employee Census 2019 - NDIS Commission Highlights

## Overview
A internal report detailing the results of the 2019 Australian Public Service (APS) Employee Census for the [[NDIS-Commission]] staff.

## Key Takeaways
- **Response Rate:** 83% (151 of 181 employees).
- **Engagement Score:** 78% (higher than the APS overall).
- **Inclusion:** 83% believe the agency is committed to creating a diverse workforce.
- **Wellbeing:** 62% index score (areas for improvement in health and wellbeing policies).
- **Leadership:** 81% believe their SES manager is of high quality.

## Areas for Action
- Internal communication and mobility within the agency were identified as areas needing improvement.
- Corruption management procedures were noted as an area where staff felt the agency could strengthen its approach.
