# Summary: NDIS Code of Conduct Poster

## Overview
A visual summary of the mandatory [[Code-of-Conduct]] that applies to all registered and unregistered [[Provider]]s and [[Worker]]s.

## The Eight Rules
1. Respect individual rights to freedom of expression and decision-making.
2. Respect the [[Privacy]] of people with disability.
3. Provide supports in a safe and competent manner.
4. Act with integrity, honesty, and transparency.
5. Promptly raise and act on concerns impacting quality and safety.
6. Prevent and respond to all forms of [[VANE]].
7. Prevent and respond to [[Sexual-Misconduct]].
8. **Pricing:** Do not charge higher prices for goods without reasonable justification (avoids price gouging).
