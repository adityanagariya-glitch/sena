# Summary: APS Employee Census 2020 - NDIS Commission Highlights

## Overview
Results from the 2020 APS Employee Census, capturing the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on [[NDIS-Commission-Staff]].

## Key Takeaways
- **Response Rate:** 85% (247 of 291 employees - showing significant agency growth).
- **COVID-19 Impact:** 49% of staff worked on activities directly related to the pandemic response.
- **Wellbeing:** Wellbeing ranking was relatively low compared to other agencies (94th of 97). 16% of staff strongly agreed they felt burned out.
- **Productivity:** 15% reported significantly improved productivity since COVID-19, despite 47% being well above capacity.

## Leadership & Communication
- Communication between SES and staff saw a decline (39% positive variance).
- Only 34% felt internal communication was effective during the crisis.
