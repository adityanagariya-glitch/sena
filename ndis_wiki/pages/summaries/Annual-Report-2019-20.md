# Summary: Annual Report 2019-2020 (Easy Read)

## Overview
A high-level summary of the [[NDIS-Commission]]'s activities and performance during the 2019-2020 financial year.

## Key Milestones
- **Black Summer Bushfires:** Issued alerts to keep participants safe and supported providers during the disaster.
- **COVID-19 Response:** Created a special team, shared tools, and monitored 166 pandemic-related complaints.
- **Royal Commissions:** Worked with both the Aged Care and Disability Royal Commissions.
- **Ann-Marie Smith Case:** Investigated the tragic death of a participant in South Australia, leading to action against her provider.

## The Year in Numbers
- **367,612** [[Participant]]s (excluding WA).
- **17,253** Registered [[Provider]]s.
- **4,469** Complaints handled.
- **311,040** Reportable incidents notified.
- **6,074** [[Behaviour-Support-Plan]]s written.
