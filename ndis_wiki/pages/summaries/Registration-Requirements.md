# Summary: Registration Requirements by Supports and Services

## Overview
This document outlines how the type of supports a [[Provider]] delivers determines which [[Practice-Standards]] they must meet and the type of [[Audit]] they must undergo.

## Key Takeaways
- **Core Module:** Applies to all registered providers (Rights, Governance, Support Provision).
- **Supplementary Modules:** Apply based on specialized services (e.g., High Intensity Daily Personal Activities, [[Behaviour-Support]], Specialist Disability Accommodation).
- **[[Audit]] Types:**
  - **Verification:** For low-risk/low-complexity services.
  - **Certification:** For complex or higher-risk services.

## Registration Classes
The document provides a detailed table mapping specific registration groups (e.g., Household Tasks, Therapeutic Supports, Specialist Housing) to the required Practice Standard modules.
