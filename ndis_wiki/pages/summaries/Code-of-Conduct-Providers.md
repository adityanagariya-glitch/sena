# Summary: NDIS Code of Conduct - Guidance for Providers

## Overview
This document provides guidance for [[Provider]]s (both registered and unregistered) on complying with the [[Code-of-Conduct]]. It translates the seven elements of the Code into practical expectations and scenarios.

## Key Takeaways
- The Code is made under the NDIS Act 2013 and is overseen by the [[NDIS-Commission]].
- It applies to all providers and their [[Worker]]s.
- Providers are responsible for the conduct of their workers and must foster an environment of safety and transparency.

## The Seven Elements
1. **Rights & Freedom:** Act with respect for [[Choice-and-Control]].
2. **[[Privacy]]:** Respect the privacy of [[Participant]]s.
3. **Safety & Competence:** Provide services with care and skill.
4. **Integrity:** Act with honesty and transparency, avoiding [[Sharp-Practices]].
5. **Quality & Safety:** Raise and act on concerns promptly via [[Complaints-Management]].
6. **Prevention of [[VANE]]:** Prevent and respond to violence, abuse, neglect, and exploitation.
7. **Sexual Misconduct:** Prevent and respond to all forms of [[Sexual-Misconduct]].
