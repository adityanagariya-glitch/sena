# Summary: Compliance and Enforcement Policy

## Overview
Describes the [[NDIS-Commission]]'s strategy for ensuring [[Provider]]s and [[Worker]]s meet their legal obligations.

## The Compliance Pyramid
The Commission uses a proportionate approach:
1. **Base (Educate & Persuade):** Training, education, and outreach.
2. **Middle (Monitor & Investigate):** Audits, site visits, and investigations.
3. **Top (Enforce):** [[Compliance-Notice]]s, [[Infringement-Notice]]s, civil penalties, revoking registration, and [[Banning-Order]]s.

## Key Mechanisms
- **Procedural Fairness:** Providers are given an opportunity to be heard before major decisions.
- **Responsive Regulation:** Strongest actions are reserved for the most serious breaches.
