# Summary: Practices Proposed to be Prohibited

## Overview
A critical document defining specific [[Restrictive-Practices]] that are considered dangerous or unethical and should be prohibited nationally.

## Prohibited Physical Restraints
- **Prone Restraint:** Subduing a person face-down.
- **Supine Restraint:** Subduing a person face-up.
- **Pin Downs:** Holding down limbs.
- **Basket Holds:** Wrapping arms around the body.
- **Takedown Techniques:** Forcing a person to the floor.
- Any restraint that inhibits respiratory or digestive functioning.

## Punitive Approaches (Prohibited)
- **Aversive Practices:** Noxious or painful experiences (e.g., cold baths, chilli powder).
- **Overcorrection:** Disproportionate punishment.
- **Denial of Key Needs:** Withholding food, possessions, or access to family/advocates.
- **Response Cost:** Punishment where a person forgoes a positive item/activity due to behaviour.
