# Summary: NDIS Commission Corporate Plan 2019-2020

## Overview
The second corporate plan, marking the milestone of expanding jurisdiction to all Australian states except WA (which joined in 2020).

## Key Takeaways
- **Transition to Maturity:** Shifting focus from initial setup to strengthening core and specific functions.
- **Regulatory Approach:** Emphasizes preventing, reporting, investigating, and responding to harm.
- **Engagement:** Focus on "Speak Up" campaigns to empower [[Participant]]s to report issues.
- **Collaboration:** Increased intelligence sharing with the [[NDIA]] and other regulators (ACCC, ASIC, AHPRA).

## Future Priorities
- Full utilization of the array of regulatory tools provided by the NDIS Act.
- Building the NDIS Commission's research, data, and analysis capability.
