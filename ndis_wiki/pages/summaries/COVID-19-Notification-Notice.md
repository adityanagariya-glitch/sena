# Summary: Notice to NDIS Commissioner of COVID-19 Related Events (March 2020)

## Overview
A formal notice issued by Commissioner Graeme Head at the start of the pandemic, mandating that [[Provider]]s notify the [[NDIS-Commission]] of COVID-19 impacts.

## Key Takeaways
- Providers must notify the Commission of changes in scale (e.g., significant changes in [[Worker]] or [[Participant]] numbers due to the virus).
- **Privacy:** Notifications must not include the names of affected persons to protect sensitive health information.
- **Reporting Timelines:** Must be submitted "as soon as practicable" after becoming aware of the change or event.
- **Purpose:** Allows the Commission to monitor market risks and support providers during the pandemic.
