# Summary: NDIS Commission Activity Report 2019-2020

## Overview
Covers the NDIS Commission's second year of operation, marked by its expansion into five new jurisdictions on 1 July 2019.

## Key Takeaways
- **Complaints:** 4,469 complaints received.
- **Registration:** 17,253 registered providers.
- **Reportable Incidents:** 311,040 notifications received.
- **COVID-19:** 166 complaints and 1,297 notifications of support changes related to the pandemic.

## Compliance Activity
- Commenced targeted compliance activity with 231 providers regarding [[URP]]s.
- Findings showed that **53%** of URPs required further action from providers to become compliant (i.e., seeking authorization and a [[Behaviour-Support-Plan]]).
