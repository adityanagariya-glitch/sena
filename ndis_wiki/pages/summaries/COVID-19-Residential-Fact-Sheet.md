# Summary: COVID-19 Participant Fact Sheet - Residential Settings

## Overview
A guide for [[Participant]]s living in residential settings on what to expect from their [[Provider]]s during a COVID-19 outbreak.

## Provider Expectations
- **Preparedness:** Providers must have plans for outbreak management and adequate PPE supplies.
- **Communication:** Must use a participant's preferred communication method and keep them informed of any changes to supports.
- **Staffing:** Ensure enough trained staff are available despite the crisis.
- **Notification:** Providers are legally required to notify the [[NDIS-Commission]] of significant support impacts.

## Safety
If a participant feels unsafe or unhappy with support quality during the pandemic, they are encouraged to contact the Commission and "Speak Up."
