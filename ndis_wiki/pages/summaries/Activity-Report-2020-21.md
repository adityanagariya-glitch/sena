# Summary: NDIS Commission Activity Report 2020-2021

## Overview
A comprehensive report on the [[NDIS-Commission]]'s operations during its third year, including its expansion into Western Australia on 1 December 2020.

## Key Statistics
- **466,619** [[Participant]]s nationally.
- **7,231** Complaints received (a significant increase from the previous year).
- **17,834** Registered [[Provider]]s.
- **1,044,851** Reportable incidents notified.
- **6,686** [[Behaviour-Support-Practitioner]]s.

## Critical Focus: Unauthorised Restrictive Practices (URPs)
- **URPs** represented **98.7%** of all reportable incidents.
- A total of **1,032,064** URPs were reported.
- The high number is due to the requirement to report every single instance of a restrictive practice until a [[Behaviour-Support-Plan]] is in place and authorized.
- **Chemical Restraint** was the most common URP type (585,847 instances).

## Compliance & Enforcement
- **22** [[Banning-Order]]s issued.
- **149** Registration refusals.
- **1,023** Education activities.
- **1,952** Corrective compliance activities.
