# Summary: NDIS Commission Compliance and Enforcement Priorities 2021-2022

## Overview
Identifies the specific high-risk areas where the [[NDIS-Commission]] targeted its enforcement actions during the 2021-2022 period.

## Core Priorities
- **Prevention of Harm:** Targeting providers/workers alleged to have caused violence, abuse, or neglect.
- **[[Incident-Management]] and Response:** Monitoring compliance with reporting obligations.
- **[[URP]]:** Targeting the unauthorized use of restricted practices.
- **Safeguards at Home:** Ensuring participants receiving 1-on-1 care at home are safe from [[VANE]].
- **COVID-19 Response:** Ensuring continuity of supports during the pandemic.
- **Mealtime Supports:** Focus on choking risks and mealtime management plans.
- **Conflicts of Interest:** Ensuring transparent advice regarding support coordination and housing.
