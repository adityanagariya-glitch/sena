# Summary: NDIS Code of Conduct - Guidance for Workers

## Overview
A practical guide for [[Worker]]s (employees, contractors, volunteers) on their personal obligations under the [[Code-of-Conduct]].

## Key Takeaways
- Workers must prioritize the safety and rights of the [[Participant]].
- Individual accountability: A worker can be found in breach of the Code even if they were following a [[Provider]]'s direction.
- Includes [[Whistle-blower]] protections for workers who report issues to the [[NDIS-Commission]].

## Practical Obligations
- **Communication:** Use accessible forms of communication to ensure the participant's will is known.
- **Professional Boundaries:** Set and maintain boundaries to prevent [[Sexual-Misconduct]].
- **Reporting:** Immediately report incidents of [[VANE]] or [[Restrictive-Practices]].
