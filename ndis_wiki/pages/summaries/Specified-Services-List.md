# Summary: List of Specified Services and Supports (Worker Screening)

## Overview
This document specifies the classes of supports and services that require a [[Worker-Screening]] check under the NDIS Rules.

## Entirely Specified Services
A wide range of registration groups are specified in their entirety, including:
- **High Intensity Daily Personal Activities**
- **Specialist Positive [[Behaviour-Support]]**
- **Community Nursing Care**
- **Early Intervention Supports**
- **Therapeutic Supports**
- **Specialised Support Coordination**

## Partially Specified Services
- **Travel/Transport:** Only specified if the services involve specialised transport to school, educational facilities, employment, or the community. public transport is excluded.

## Regulatory Context
These categories define the scope of the [[Worker]] screening obligations for [[Provider]]s.
