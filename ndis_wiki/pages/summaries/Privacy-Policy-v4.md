# Summary: NDIS Commission Privacy Policy (Version 4.0)

## Overview
A detailed policy outlining how the [[NDIS-Commission]] handles personal and sensitive information in accordance with the **Privacy Act 1988** and the **NDIS Act 2013**.

## Key Takeaways
- **[[Protected-Commission-Information]]:** Most information held by the Commission falls under strict secrecy provisions; unauthorized disclosure is a criminal offence.
- **Collection:** Information is collected to perform core functions (Registration, Complaints, etc.) and may be obtained from third parties like the [[NDIA]] or law enforcement.
- **AI Transparency:** The Commission may use AI technologies for data analysis.
- **Storage:** Data is stored securely, including on systems managed by partner agencies (Services Australia and DSS).

## Governance
- **[[Privacy-Champion]]:** Provides leadership on strategic privacy issues.
- **[[Privacy-Officers]]:** Handle enquiries, complaints, and access requests.
