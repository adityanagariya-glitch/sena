# Summary: NDIS Commission Corporate Plan 2018-2019

## Overview
The inaugural corporate plan for the [[NDIS-Commission]], setting the strategic direction for its first four years of operation (2018-2022).

## Vision & Purpose
- **Vision:** People with disability receive quality and safe supports and services under the NDIS.
- **Purpose:** To deliver nationally consistent, responsive, and effective regulation of NDIS providers.

## Key Design Principles
1. **Informed Consumers:** Supporting [[Participant]]s to access high-quality services.
2. **Agile Regulator:** Responsive to sector risk and quality.
3. **Proportionate Regulation:** Managing non-compliance based on risk.
4. **Inclusive Workplace:** Creating a productive and diverse workplace for [[NDIS-Commission-Staff]].

## The [[Quality-and-Safeguarding-Framework]]
The plan is the key vehicle for implementing the national Framework, replacing fragmented state and territory systems with a single national regulator.
