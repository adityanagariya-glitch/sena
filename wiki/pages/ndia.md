# NDIA

The **National Disability Insurance Agency** is the Australian statutory body that administers the NDIS. It sets provider registration rules, pricing, compliance requirements, and audit criteria.

## Key outputs relevant to SENA

- **NDIS Practice Standards** — quality requirements for registered providers
- **NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits** — what providers can charge
- **NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission** — separate body enforcing conduct and safeguards (co-regulator)
- **Operational Guidelines** — detailed rules on plan administration, claims, disputes

## Why this matters

Case notes generated by SENA must satisfy whatever documentation standard the participant's provider is registered under. SENA's [[human-in-the-loop]] approval step is what keeps provider accountability intact — the AI drafts, the human signs off.
