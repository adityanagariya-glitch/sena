# NDIS Overview

The **National Disability Insurance Scheme** is an Australian federal program providing funding for supports and services to people with permanent and significant disability. It replaced a fragmented state-based system in 2013.

## Actors

- **[[ndia]]** — National Disability Insurance Agency; administers NDIS, sets rules, manages provider registration
- **Participants** — people eligible for NDIS funding (about 700k+ Australians)
- **Service providers** — registered organisations delivering supports (SENA's customers are in this group)
- **Support workers** — frontline staff employed by providers; do the day-to-day work with participants
- **Plan managers / coordinators** — help participants manage their NDIS plans

## How it works

1. Participant receives an **NDIS plan** with a budget and goals
2. Plan funds **supports**: e.g., personal care, community access, therapy, assistive tech
3. Participant engages **providers** to deliver those supports
4. Providers deliver services, log what happened ([[case-notes]]), bill the NDIS
5. Everything is auditable — NDIS Quality & Safeguards Commission oversees compliance

## Why this matters for SENA

SENA's customers are service providers. Their support workers generate large volumes of case notes daily. Writing those notes is time-consuming and error-prone. SENA automates the drafting via voice ([[flow-b-voice-dictation]]) while keeping humans in the approval loop ([[human-in-the-loop]]).

Because provider work touches participant health, finance, and safeguarding data, the data handling bar is high — see [[ndis-compliance]].
